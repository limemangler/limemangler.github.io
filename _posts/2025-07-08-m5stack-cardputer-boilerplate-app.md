### About the Cardputer Boilerplate App
---
If you have tried making custom firmware for any device, you will know how complicated it can be. When I saw the Cardputer for the first time I was stoked to try it out, but after running through a few of the apps, I was ready to see if I could build something on my own. 

*Maybe I'm spoiled, but the lack of documentation for this device was pretty wild!! There are plenty of people out there making stuff, but there's not much in the way of beginner friendly, learning focused apps.*

So... here we are. I built my first ever program in C++ and it is pretty rough, but it works! I wanted to have something simple, that was pretty well commented and could be broken into pieces to be reused in a new app. 

**And so the bp-app was born...**
You can check it out over on github at [bp-app](https://github.com/limemangler/bp-app) but please keep in mind that this is meant to be a **barebones, no-frills experience** to prototype a new app for the M5Stack Cardputer.

I decided that the best approach for my future needs would probably be a simple splash screen, a welcome menu with a couple of default options, and to integrate the basic text input app from the [demo code](https://github.com/m5stack/M5Cardputer/tree/master/examples/Basic/keyboard/inputText) to show how to use more complex functionality.

I hope this can be of use to someone out there!
